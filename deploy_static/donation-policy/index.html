Donation Policy

CollabNET is free to use. When publication data is loaded, each export starts after a 5-minute wait. You may support the project with a voluntary gift; doing so skips the wait for the rest of that browser session.

Who receives it

Gifts go to Vishvesh Karthik, the individual developer of CollabNET (vishveshkarthik@gmail.com), through Buy Me a Coffee.

What a gift is (and is not)

  • A voluntary personal gift of any amount, one-time or monthly, made on Buy Me a Coffee. Buy Me a Coffee's own terms and fees apply to the payment.
  • It is not a purchase of data, of a service or of a licence, and it creates no contract for support.
  • No 80G or other tax (exemption) receipt is issued.
  • Exports are always free after the 5-minute wait, whether or not you give.

Refunds

Gifts are non-refundable, except as provided by the Buy Me a Coffee refund policy. If a gift is refunded, the export wait applies again (the 5-minute countdown; exports are never blocked).

Monthly support

Monthly support can be cancelled at any time on Buy Me a Coffee. It skips the wait until the end of the period you have paid for and stops doing so after that. Before subscribing, use “Already a monthly supporter? Check” in the export dialog so you don't subscribe twice.

How the wait is skipped, and privacy

The export dialog shows a short code (for example CN-7KQ4X2). Put it in your Buy Me a Coffee message; when Buy Me a Coffee notifies CollabNET's server (a Cloudflare Worker), your open CollabNET tab unlocks. Buy Me a Coffee's privacy policy applies to your payment data. The CollabNET Worker stores no names, messages or raw email addresses, only:

  • a salted SHA-256 hash of the payer email, so “Check by email” and “Already a monthly supporter? Check” can work (one-time gifts: 60 minutes; monthly support: until the end of the paid period plus 3 days);
  • the one-time code and whether it was paid/claimed (2 hours);
  • a hashed payment id and refund flag (about 13 hours), and processed webhook ids (7 days, to ignore repeats);
  • short-lived request counters for rate limiting, kept only in the server's memory (about a minute; the IP is hashed, never written to storage);
  • one anonymous counter of possible duplicate monthly subscriptions (no personal data).

Every stored item expires automatically. The app keeps the unlock only in the open page's memory: nothing is written to cookies, browser storage or .cnet files, so a reload starts with the wait again.
